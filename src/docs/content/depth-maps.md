# Depth Maps

AMVerge can create a depth map of your clips after exporting them, using Depth-Anything-V2. A depth map shows how near or far each part of the frame is.

## Before you begin

Turn on **Depth Map Pass** in **Settings > Features**. It downloads what it needs the first time. See [Turn On AI Features](/docs/ai-features).

## Steps

1. In **Settings**, open the export settings and turn on **Depth map pass**.
2. Choose a **Depth model** and a **Colormap**, or turn on **Grayscale**.
3. Select your clips and click **Export Now**.
4. If you selected two or more clips, choose which ones get a depth map, then click **Export**.

AMVerge exports your clips, then creates the depth maps as separate files.
