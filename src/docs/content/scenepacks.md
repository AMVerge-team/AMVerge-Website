# Scenepacks

A Scenepack is a named collection of clips. Use one to keep your favorite clips together, even when they come from different episodes.

## Before you begin

Turn on Scenepacks in **Settings > Features**. It doesn't need a download.

## Add clips to a Scenepack

1. Select one or more clips in your Library.
2. Click **Add to Scenepack**.
3. Choose **Existing** to pick a pack, or **Create New** to name a new one.
4. Click the add button.

AMVerge saves its own copy of each clip into the pack.

## Use a Scenepack

Open **Scenepacks** in the sidebar and select a pack. Its clips appear in the grid, where you can preview and [export](/docs/export-clips) them as usual.
