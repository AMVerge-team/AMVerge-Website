# Browse and Select Clips

After you import an episode, its scenes appear as a grid of tiles.

## Preview scenes

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| Hover over a tile | Plays that clip |
| Click a tile | Plays it in the preview panel |
| Click **Preview All** | Plays every visible tile |

To hear audio when you hover, turn on **Audio Playback Hover** in **Settings > General**.

## Select clips

| Shortcut | Result |
|---|---|
| **Ctrl + Click** | Selects or deselects one clip |
| **Shift + Click** | Selects a range of clips |

The toolbar shows how many clips you selected. Click the **X** beside the count to clear the selection.

## Change the grid size

Hold **Ctrl** and scroll over the grid to change the number of columns, or use the **+** and **-** buttons at the top of the window.

## Organize episodes

The episode panel on the left lists your imported episodes. You can group them into folders, and each episode reopens where you left off.

## Choose a preview language

For videos with more than one audio track, use **Preview Language** in the export panel to choose which track clips play with. Exports put that track first.

## Move around quickly

Press **Esc** to open quick navigation, then jump to any episode, Scenepack, action or setting.

## On macOS

Use **Cmd** instead of **Ctrl** for these shortcuts.
